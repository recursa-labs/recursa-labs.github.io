recursa labs.

An independent research lab focused on continual learning in AI systems.
Based in Bangalore, India.

01Focus
  • Build self-learning agents that improve as they work.
  • Learn through experience: observe, try, reflect, and adapt.
  • Turn mistakes into lasting lessons, with the aim of never repeating them.
  • Carry useful knowledge from familiar tasks into new situations.
02Strategies
  • L0: Build world models through observation and experimentation.
  • L1: Turn task experience into reusable domain skills.
  • L2: Learn how to learn by revising learning procedures.
  • L3: Score gains in learning efficiency and improve the learning process itself.
03Values
  • Understand how intelligence works, and build systems from those insights.
  • Follow curiosity, question assumptions, and stay close to new research.
  • Test ideas through controlled, reproducible experiments.
  • Explore domains from ARC-AGI puzzles to long-horizon tasks such as Reddit marketing.
04Roadmap
  • Complete self-learning experiments and establish a concrete architecture.
  • Build a general-purpose harness that anyone can use.
